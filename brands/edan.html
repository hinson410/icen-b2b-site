Why ICEN

Why source Edan equipment through ICEN

Veterinary-specific specification

Veterinary imaging and laboratory equipment is specified differently from human clinical equipment - species mix, caseload and the physical constraints of a consulting room all change the answer. ICEN publishes the veterinary models it supplies rather than listing them under a general medical catalogue.

Documentation matched to the destination

CE and ISO documentation is available per model. Because availability and market authorization depend on the destination market, ICEN confirms the documentation set for each model and each destination instead of assuming one global package.

After-sales

A five-person after-sales team handles technical questions, warranty handling and spare-part requests after delivery. Warranty terms are quoted per project.

Specification

How to specify veterinary equipment

Species mix

Small companion animals, mixed practice and large-animal work pull transducer and preset requirements in different directions.

Caseload and throughput

Daily scan and sample volume determines whether a portable or cart-based platform is the better fit.

Where the equipment lives

Portable systems suit mobile and multi-room use; cart systems suit a dedicated imaging room.

Consumables and service

Reagents, probes and the service route belong in the same quotation as the capital equipment.
