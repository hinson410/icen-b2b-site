Overview

What ICEN supplies from Mindray

Mindray is a Chinese medical equipment manufacturer covering patient monitoring, diagnostic imaging, in-vitro diagnostics, life support and anesthesia. ICEN supplies Mindray equipment across the clinical categories listed below, with the model range published rather than described in general terms so buyers can compare specifications before requesting a quotation.

Categories are grouped by where the equipment is used rather than by manufacturer segment, because hospital projects are usually specified department by department.

Specification

How to specify Mindray equipment

Start with the clinical requirement

Patient population, department and case mix determine the platform before brand or tier.

Confirm the configuration, not just the model

Confirm documentation early

Agree the service route

Who installs, who trains and who handles warranty should be settled in the quotation rather than after delivery.
