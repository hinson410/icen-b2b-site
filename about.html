Quality & Compliance

Built on a Controlled Quality System

Medical devices demand discipline. Our quality system governs every stage from incoming material to final packing.

  • Quality management under ISO 13485 practices
  • Incoming inspection of all materials and components
  • In-process quality checks on every production line
  • 100% functional testing before shipment
  • Batch traceability with complete production records
  • Regular calibration of test and measurement equipment
