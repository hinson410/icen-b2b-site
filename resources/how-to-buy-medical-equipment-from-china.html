Buying Guide

How to Buy Medical Equipment from China

A practical guide for international buyers sourcing medical equipment from China: product selection, documentation, quotation, shipping and after-sales support.

Buying Guide

1. Define the project

Start with country, clinical department, intended use, quantity, budget range and required delivery timeline.

Buying Guide

2. Build a precise equipment list

Use manufacturer, model, configuration, accessories and consumables fields so suppliers quote the same scope.

Buying Guide

3. Check documentation

Confirm product documents, certificates, manuals and destination-market requirements before ordering.

Buying Guide

4. Compare more than unit price

Evaluate configuration, warranty, service, spare parts, documentation, lead time and export capability.

Buying Guide

5. Confirm logistics

Agree delivery terms, packing, shipping method and the documents required by the destination.

Buying Guide

6. Keep after-sales expectations clear

Confirm installation support, training, technical contact, warranty handling and spare-parts communication.
