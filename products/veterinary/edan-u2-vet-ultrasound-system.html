Product Overview

Four probes connected, on a trolley

The U2 Vet is Edan’s cost-effective trolley system. It is published on edan.com under the title U2 Prime Edition VET, and the catalogue model is U2 Vet.

The configuration that distinguishes it is the four active transducer sockets. Compared with the two exchangeable connectors on the U50, four sockets mean a mixed practice can keep convex, linear, micro-convex and phased array probes all live and switch without unplugging.

The display is 15 inches with a 19-inch option, and the imaging set includes spatial compounding, multi-beam acquisition, speckle reduction and phase-inversion harmonic imaging, with SmartPreSet for starting presets.

Applications

  • Mixed practice imaging
  • Abdominal and reproduction scanning
  • Superficial and musculoskeletal work with linear probes
  • Basic cardiac imaging

Who is this product for?

  • Mixed and large animal practice
  • Small animal clinics
  • Referral practice
  • Clinics wanting four probes on one cart

FAQ

Common questions about Edan U2 Vet Veterinary Ultrasound System

Why does the model name differ?

Edan publishes this system as U2 Prime Edition VET on edan.com, and the catalogue lists it as U2 Vet. Confirm the exact variant and probe package when you order.

How many probes can be connected?

Four active transducer sockets, so a convex, linear, micro-convex and phased array probe can all stay connected.

Is a 19-inch screen available?

Yes, the 15-inch high-resolution display is standard and a 19-inch display is offered as an option.

What imaging modes are supported?

Edan lists spatial compounding, multi-beam, speckle reduction and phase-inversion harmonic imaging, and describes the system generically as colour and Doppler capable. A detailed mode list is not published on the product page.

How does it compare with the U60 Vet?

Both are 15-inch systems. The U2 is a trolley with four active sockets; the U60 is a desktop unit with two simultaneously connected ports and triplex imaging.

What is not published?

Screen resolution, weight, dimensions, battery time and whether a needle guide is included are not stated. Ask ICEN for the datasheet.
