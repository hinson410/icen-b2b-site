Product Overview

An in-house reference bench rather than a panel reader

Mindray positions the BS-240 Vet as a private reference laboratory for in-house use. It is a random-access benchtop analyzer rather than a cartridge reader, so the menu is continuous and the operator is not limited to what fits on one disc.

Capacity is the practical difference: up to 80 sample positions and up to 80 reagent positions, of which 40 are fixed and 40 interchangeable. That mix lets a laboratory keep its high-volume reagents permanently loaded while still rotating seasonal or low-volume tests.

The reaction volume floor is 100 microlitres, which matters for paediatric and exotic samples as much as for reagent cost. The probe carries liquid-level detection, collision protection and reagent pre-warming, and Mindray specifies an upgraded automatic washing system for low carryover with low water consumption.

Applications

  • Routine biochemistry in a clinic laboratory
  • Pre-anaesthetic and geriatric screening
  • Small-volume and exotic samples
  • Continuous menu work in a referral laboratory

Who is this product for?

  • Referral hospitals
  • Large clinics with an in-house laboratory
  • Mixed practice laboratories
  • Laboratories running a broad, changing menu

FAQ

Common questions about Mindray BS-240 Vet Chemistry Analyzer

What throughput does it offer?

Mindray does not publish a tests-per-hour figure on the BS-240 Vet product page, and dealer listings differ. Ask ICEN for the official datasheet before you compare it against a tender requirement.

Why does the 40 + 40 reagent split matter?

Forty positions stay fixed for the reagents you always run, and forty are interchangeable so seasonal, exotic or low-volume assays can be added without unloading the routine menu.

How much sample does one test need?

The minimum reaction volume is 100 microlitres. Actual sample uptake per assay depends on the parameters, so ask ICEN for the parameter sheet for your menu.

Can it run exotic or avian chemistry?

The analyzer is menu-driven rather than panel-driven, so capability depends on which veterinary assays you load. Ask ICEN to confirm the assay list for avian and exotic species for your configuration.

How is carryover controlled?

Mindray specifies an upgraded automatic washing system designed for low carryover with low water consumption, together with a probe that has liquid level detection and collision protection.

How does it differ from the VetXpert C5?

The C5 runs fixed panels from a cartridge for fast in-consultation results. The BS-240 Vet is random access with 160 loading positions across sample and reagent, built for continuous menu work.
