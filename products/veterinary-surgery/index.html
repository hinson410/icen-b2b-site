Veterinary Surgery & Imaging

ICEN supplies two Mindray Animal Medical families for the veterinary operating theatre — the VeLED LED surgical and examination lighting range, and the Vetina endoscopy series covering the CS7, CS5 Ultra and CS5 rigid towers and the ES3 flexible system — for referral hospitals, surgical referral practice and teaching hospitals.

Veterinary analysers, monitors and ultrasound are listed under veterinary diagnostics.

Category overview

Surgery and imaging equipment for a veterinary theatre

Theatre equipment is usually specified later than diagnostics, and it is where the room itself constrains the product: ceiling height, rotation radius and working distance decide whether a given light head can be installed at all.

VeLED is the LED surgical and examination lighting range. Mindray publishes it as a series rather than as numbered sub-models, with a 170 to 250 mm adjustable light field, electronic focusing over 0.8 to 1.2 m, an IP54 lamp head and a 100,000-hour service life.

Vetina is the endoscopy family: CS7, CS5 Ultra and CS5 rigid towers plus the ES3 flexible system, with 4K imaging up to 3840 by 2160, anti-fogging endoscopes, built-in USB recording and an integrated veterinary insufflator and light source.

Veterinary diagnostics covers analysers, monitors and ultrasound; this category covers the theatre itself.

FAQ

Common questions about veterinary surgery equipment

Which veterinary surgery and imaging products can ICEN supply?

ICEN supplies the Mindray VeLED veterinary surgical and examination lighting range and the Vetina veterinary endoscopy series, which covers the CS7, CS5 Ultra and CS5 rigid towers and the ES3 flexible system.

Is VeLED the same as HyLED?

No. HyLED is Mindray’s human clinical surgical lighting. VeLED is published on Mindray’s veterinary site and is specified for veterinary theatres.

Does Vetina exist as a single model?

No. Vetina is a family. CS7, CS5 Ultra and CS5 are rigid towers and ES3 is the flexible endoscopy system. Ask ICEN to quote against the configuration you need.

Can I buy a light or a scope on its own?

Yes. Surgical lights and endoscopy towers are separate purchases, and an endoscopy quotation normally includes the camera, scope set and monitor. Send the procedures you intend to perform and ICEN will specify the scope inventory.

Specifying a veterinary theatre?

Send the room dimensions and the procedures you intend to perform, and ICEN will confirm the lighting installation and the endoscope inventory before quoting.
