Product Overview

A mobile general-imaging system for primary care

The Consona N5 is positioned by Mindray for primary care and general imaging. It is a mobile general-purpose system rather than a cart-mounted premium platform, which is what makes it suitable for clinics, smaller hospitals and shared imaging rooms.

Mindray’s official material for the Consona N5 does not publish a monitor size, a transducer list, dimensions, weight or power specification. Distributor pages in the market quote figures for those rows, but they cannot be traced to an official source, so they are not reproduced here.

    FAQ

    Questions buyers ask about the Mindray Consona N5

    Is the Consona N5 a portable scanner or a trolley system?

    It is a mobile general-purpose ultrasound system for primary care and general imaging. Some marketplace listings describe a portable laptop-class device with a large LCD and a low weight; that description does not match Mindray’s own positioning, and we do not publish those figures.

    Why are the monitor size and probe list missing?

    Because Mindray does not publish them for this model. The official product page and the official brochure text do not state a monitor size or a transducer list, so we leave those rows marked as not published.

    How do we choose transducers then?

    Tell us the clinical applications you need to cover and we will confirm the transducer set with the official configuration documentation for your market.

    Is the Consona N5 suitable for a small clinic?

    Yes — primary care and general imaging is exactly how Mindray positions it, which makes it appropriate for clinics and smaller hospitals.

    Does ICEN supply the official datasheet and regulatory documents?

    Yes. We supply the official documentation for your configuration, and the model carries an official regulatory record which we can point you to.
