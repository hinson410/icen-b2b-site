Product Overview

A shared primary-care ultrasound platform with the DC-28 imaging feature set

The Mindray DC-28 belongs to the ultrasound platform that Mindray cleared as the DC-30/DC-32/DC-28/DC-26/DC-25 diagnostic ultrasound family, a general-purpose, mobile, software-controlled system. Mindray states the family is applicable for adults, pregnant women, pediatric patients and neonates.

Imaging coverage spans B, M, pulsed-wave Doppler, color Doppler and amplitude Doppler, with combined modes of B+M, PW+B, color+B, power+B, PW+color+B and power+PW+B. Tissue Harmonic Imaging and biopsy guidance are available across the family, and the DC-28 adds the advanced feature set the manufacturer markets for primary care: Auto IMT for intima-media thickness, iScape panoramic imaging, Smart3D and real-time 4D, elastography, and contrast imaging for liver studies.

ICEN supplies the DC-28 to hospitals, imaging centres and healthcare distributors. Because Mindray does not currently publish a DC-28 specification sheet on mindray.com, we confirm the exact configuration, transducer selection and supporting technical documents with our team before quotation.

FAQ

Common questions about Mindray DC-28 Color Doppler Ultrasound System

What is the Mindray DC-28 used for?

It is a general-purpose color Doppler ultrasound system for adults, pregnant women, pediatric patients and neonates, covering fetal, abdominal, pediatric, small organ, cephalic, trans-rectal, trans-vaginal, musculo-skeletal, cardiac, peripheral vessel and urology examinations.

Which imaging modes does the DC-28 support?

B, M, pulsed-wave Doppler, color Doppler and amplitude Doppler. Combined modes include B+M, PW+B, color+B, power+B, PW+color+B and power+PW+B.

Does the DC-28 include 4D imaging and elastography?

Yes, both are part of the DC-28 feature set: Smart3D and real-time 4D imaging (a volume-compatible transducer is required for 4D), plus elastography and contrast imaging for liver studies.
