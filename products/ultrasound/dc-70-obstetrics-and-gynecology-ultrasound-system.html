Product Overview

An OB/GYN ultrasound system with on-system measurement automation

The Mindray DC-70 is an obstetrics and gynecology ultrasound system built on the X-Engine, which integrates GPU and CPU multi-core parallel processing for faster imaging processing and fast 3D/4D imaging. The system is specified with a 21.5-inch or 23.8-inch Full HD monitor on an eagle-wing floating arm and a 13.3-inch ultra-slim multi-gesture touch screen with angle adjustment.

The OB/GYN workflow tools are the core of the platform. One-click Smart Planes CNS handles plane detection and measurements across MSP, TCP, TTP and TVP with BPD, HC, OFD, TCD, CM and LVW measurements. Smart FLC provides automatic follicle counting, and Smart Pelvic supports pelvic-floor assessment.

Transducer options include single crystal volume, convex and phased-array probes plus ComboWave linear transducers with 3T technology for resolution and penetration. Glazing Flow 3D visualises the smallest vessels in colour Doppler, and iLive volume rendering is powered by the X-Engine. ICEN supports hospitals, women's health clinics and distributors with configuration, documentation and export coordination.

Applications

  • Obstetrics and gynecology imaging
  • Fetal central nervous system plane detection and measurement with Smart Planes CNS
