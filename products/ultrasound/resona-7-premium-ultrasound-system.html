Product Overview

Premium imaging platform with ZST+ and V Flow

The Mindray Resona 7 is a premium diagnostic ultrasound system built on the ZST+ platform, which applies ZONE Sonography Technology to channel-data processing together with Advanced Acoustic Acquisition, Dynamic Pixel Focusing, Sound Speed Compensation and Total Recall Imaging. In practice this means image formation is handled from channel data rather than conventional line-by-line beamforming.

V Flow (Vector Flow) visualises complex vascular hemodynamics independent of insonation angle, at ultra-high frame rates, with colour-coded velocity magnitude and direction. For contrast and tissue assessment the system offers UWN+ (Ultra-Wideband Non-linear Plus) CEUS using second-harmonic and non-linear fundamental signals for higher sensitivity at lower mechanical index, plus Sound Touch Elastography for real-time 2D shear-wave quantitative elastography.

Clinical automation includes fully automatic Smart Planes CNS plane detection with measurements (MSP, TCP, TTP, TVP; BPD, HC, OFD, TCD, CM, LVW), one-touch Smart Face and Smart Pelvic. Ergonomics include gesture-based multi-touch operation, a swiveling control panel and a 3-level gel warmer. ICEN supports hospitals, imaging centres and distributors with configuration, documentation and export coordination.

Applications

  • General imaging and diagnostic assessment
  • Obstetrics and gynaecology workflow support with Smart Planes CNS
  • Vascular assessment with angle-independent V Flow imaging
  • Contrast-enhanced and elastography-based assessment (UWN+, Sound Touch Elastography)
