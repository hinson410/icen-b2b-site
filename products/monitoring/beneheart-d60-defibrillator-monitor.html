Product Overview

A next-generation defibrillator/monitor with pre-hospital decision support

The Mindray BeneHeart D60 takes the next-generation BeneHeart platform further than the D30 by adding decision-support tools on top of 360 J manual defibrillation, AED and pacing. QShock brings the unit to shock-ready in less than five seconds from power on, with a two-second power-on time and 2.5-second ECG recovery.

A 9-inch capacitive full-touch screen at 1200 x 1020 resolution carries auto-brightness and gesture control, with a physical knob and hard keys retained for critical actions. Beyond ECG, SpO2, NIBP, CO2, IBP and temperature monitoring, the D60 adds 12-lead ECG analysis with critical value detection and MI location, point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS) with a phased array probe, an infrared ear thermometer, and GCS, EWS and HEART scoring with TBI warning. A built-in 6-channel printer uses 110 mm paper.

For transport the D60 is rated IP55, survives a 1.5 m drop on six surfaces without a carrycase and 3 m with one, and operates from -20 to +55 degC. It connects through 4G to a central monitoring system and over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth to a medical pad and ePCR, with the M-IoT Device Manager for fleet oversight. The unit weighs 4.2 kg with battery, resists 49 cleaners, and its interface withstands more than 100 kg of pulling force. ICEN supplies the BeneHeart D60 to hospitals, emergency services and distributors, and confirms configuration and market requirements before quotation.
