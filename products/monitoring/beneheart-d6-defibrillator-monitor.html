Product Overview

A fully featured defibrillator/monitor for emergency and critical care

The Mindray BeneHeart D6 adds monitoring depth to the BeneHeart defibrillator platform. Manual defibrillation covers 21 selectable settings from 1 J to 360 J with a biphasic truncated exponential waveform and automatic impedance compensation, and charges to 200 J in less than three seconds or 360 J in less than seven seconds. Synchronized cardioversion delivers energy within 60 ms of the QRS peak.

Monitoring is the main difference from the D3: an 8.4-inch TFT colour display at 800 x 600 shows up to four waveform channels, and the unit monitors 3/5/12-lead ECG with respiration, SpO2, NIBP, EtCO2 (both Sidestream and Microstream), two IBP channels and two-channel temperature. A thermal recorder is built in and prints on 50 mm or 80 mm paper.

Noninvasive pacing runs from 30 to 210 ppm at 0 to 200 mA. Two 15.1 V / 5600 mAh lithium-ion packs can be fitted, giving roughly 400 shocks at 360 J, 12 hours of monitoring or 9 hours of pacing. Data leaves the unit over 5G or 2.4G Wi-Fi using IHE HL7 with DHCP, and the D6 integrates with a central monitoring system and the Pre-Hospital Emergency Information System. ICEN supplies the BeneHeart D6 to hospitals, emergency services and distributors, and confirms configuration and market requirements before quotation.

FAQ

Common questions about Mindray BeneHeart D6 Defibrillator / Monitor

How does the BeneHeart D6 differ from the D3?

The D6 has a larger 8.4-inch display with up to 4 waveform channels, adds 12-lead ECG, 2-channel IBP and 2-channel temperature monitoring, includes a built-in thermal recorder, and accepts two battery packs instead of one.

What defibrillation energy does the D6 deliver?

Manual mode uses 21 selectable settings from 1 J to 360 J with a biphasic truncated exponential waveform and impedance compensation. Charge time is under 3 s to 200 J and under 7 s to 360 J.

Which CO2 options are available?

Both Sidestream CO2 and Microstream CO2 are supported, alongside SpO2, NIBP, 3/5/12-lead ECG, 2-channel IBP and 2-channel temperature.

How long does the D6 run on battery?

With two 15.1 V / 5600 mAh lithium-ion packs the unit is rated for about 400 shocks at 360 J, 12 hours of monitoring or 9 hours of pacing.

Does the D6 integrate with hospital systems?

Yes. It transmits over 5G/2.4G Wi-Fi using IHE HL7 with DHCP, exports data via USB, and integrates with a central monitoring system and the Pre-Hospital Emergency Information System (PHEIS).
