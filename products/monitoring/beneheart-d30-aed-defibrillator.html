Product Overview

A next-generation AED/defibrillator built for speed and hard conditions

The Mindray BeneHeart D30 is the transport-focused member of the next-generation BeneHeart family: manual defibrillation, AED and pacing in one unit, with a maximum manual energy of 360 J. QShock technology is specified to reach shock-ready in less than five seconds from power on, and the unit powers on in two seconds with the ECG recovered in 2.5 seconds, so treatment is not delayed by the device.

Operation is built around an 8-inch capacitive full-touch screen at 1024 x 768 with auto-brightness and gesture control, while a physical knob and hard keys are kept for the actions that must work with gloves or under stress. Monitoring covers ECG, SpO2, NIBP and CO2. CPR process feedback reports depth, rate, recoil, interruption time and chest compression fraction, with CPR effect evaluation based on Mindray SpO2.

The unit is rated IP55 for water and dust and survives a 0.75 m drop on all six sides, with a bedrail hook for transport. The main unit with battery weighs 4.2 kg. It stores 1,000 event sets, 200 hours of trend data, 1,000 auto test reports and 8 hours of voice recording, and connects through the M-Connect IT solution and the M-IoT Device Manager. The defibrillation cable is tested over 400,000 bending cycles and the housing resists 49 disinfectants. ICEN supplies the BeneHeart D30 to hospitals, emergency services and distributors, and confirms configuration and market requirements before quotation.

FAQ

Common questions about Mindray BeneHeart D30 AED / Defibrillator

What is QShock on the BeneHeart D30?

QShock is the charging and start-up design that brings the unit to shock-ready in less than 5 seconds from power on. Power on time is 2 seconds and ECG recovery time is 2.5 seconds.

What modes does the D30 support?

Manual defibrillation, AED and pacing, plus monitoring - a 4-in-1 configuration.

Which parameters does it monitor?

ECG, SpO2, NIBP and CO2.

Does the D30 provide CPR feedback?

Yes. It reports CPR depth, rate, recoil, interruption time and chest compression fraction (CCF), and evaluates CPR effect using Mindray SpO2 (CQI).

How durable is the D30?

It is rated IP55 for water and dust resistance and is protected against a 0.75 m drop on all six sides. It resists 49 disinfectants, and the defibrillation cable is tested over 400,000 bending cycles.
