Patient Monitoring & Cardiac Care

ICEN supplies eight Mindray cardiac care devices — the BeneHeart R300 and R12 electrocardiographs, the BeneHeart D3, D6, D30 and D60 defibrillator/monitors, the BeneHeart C2 AED and the VS 600 vital signs monitor — for emergency departments, wards, operating theatres and outpatient clinics.

Category Overview

Patient Monitoring & Cardiac Care supplied by ICEN

The range covers three jobs. BeneHeart R300 and R12 are resting electrocardiographs: R300 is a 12-lead unit with a 5-inch colour screen weighing about 1.28 kg with battery, and R12 is a 12-channel unit with an 8-inch screen, built-in thermal printer and storage for up to 800 records. BeneHeart D3, D6, D30 and D60 are defibrillator/monitors for crash carts, emergency rooms and critical care, stepping from the 7-inch D3 and 8.4-inch D6 through the 8-inch capacitive-touch D30 to the 9-inch D60, all delivering up to 360 J biphasic defibrillation with AED and pacing modes. BeneHeart C2 is a standalone AED using QShock technology specified for a first shock in under 8 seconds. VS 600 covers routine vital signs on a 5.7-inch display.
