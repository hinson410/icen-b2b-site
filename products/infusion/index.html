Infusion Systems

ICEN supplies six Mindray BeneFusion infusion devices — the eVP and nVP infusion pumps, the eSP and nSP syringe pumps, and the eDS and nDS infusion supervision systems — for accurate fluid and drug delivery in general wards, ICU and operating theatres.

Category Overview

Infusion Systems supplied by ICEN

The BeneFusion range has two series, each pairing an infusion pump, a syringe pump and a supervision system so a bed or department can be monitored from one point. In the e-series, eVP is a volumetric infusion pump accurate within ±5% and eSP a syringe pump accurate within ±1.8% across a 0.01–2300 mL/h flow range with 15 occlusion alarm levels and a 3.5-inch colour display; eDS supervises 2, 4 or 6 pump bays with a 4-bay extension for up to 16 slots over Wi-Fi. In the n-series, nVP delivers 0.10–2300 mL/h with a 7-inch full touchscreen and air-bubble detection from 15 µL, nSP offers ±1.8% syringe accuracy, and nDS provides the same pump-bay supervision with an OLED controller display.

ICEN lists 6 models in this category, including Mindray BeneFusion eVP Infusion Pump, Mindray BeneFusion eSP Syringe Pump and Mindray BeneFusion eDS Infusion Supervision System. ICEN provides sourcing and project support for distributors, hospitals and healthcare programs, and can confirm configuration, documentation and destination-market requirements before quotation.
