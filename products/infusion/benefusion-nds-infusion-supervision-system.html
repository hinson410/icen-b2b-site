Product Overview

A scalable docking station with an OLED controller and HL7 connectivity

The Mindray BeneFusion nDS is the n Series infusion supervision system. One controller runs 2, 4 or 6 pump bays, and an extension shelf adds four bays at a time, scaling the stack from 2 up to 24 slots - the largest expansion in the ICEN infusion range.

The controller carries an OLED display of 3 inches or more at 256x64 pixels, so station status is readable directly at the dock rather than only on each pump. The nDS takes both n Series and e Series pumps, which means a department standardising on n Series can keep existing e Series devices in the same station.

Connectivity is the differentiator for larger sites: Wi-Fi IEEE 802.11a/b/g/n/ac, two USB ports and a network connector, with HL7 integration to HIS, CIS and EMR systems so infusion data reaches the hospital record.

The dock is secured in the Medical Supply Unit. Weight depends on configuration: no more than 2.9 kg for 2 bays, 3.9 kg for 4 bays and 5.4 kg for 6 bays.

Applications

  • ICU and critical care ventilation
  • Invasive and non-invasive respiratory support (NIV, nCPAP)
  • Adult, pediatric and neonate ventilation
  • Weaning and lung recruitment workflows

FAQ

Common questions about Mindray BeneFusion nDS Infusion Supervision System

How far can the BeneFusion nDS scale?

From 2 to 24 slots. The controller provides 2, 4 or 6 pump bays and an extension shelf adds four bays at a time.

Can e Series pumps dock in the nDS station?

Yes - the nDS accepts both n Series and e Series pumps, so a station can mix them.

What kind of display does the nDS controller use?

An OLED display of 3 inches or larger at 256 x 64 pixels or more.

Does the nDS connect to the hospital record system?

Yes - it supports HL7 integration to HIS, CIS and EMR, over Wi-Fi IEEE 802.11a/b/g/n/ac, two USB ports and a network connector.

How much does the nDS weigh?

Up to 2.9 kg for the 2-bay dock, 3.9 kg for 4 bays and 5.4 kg for 6 bays.
