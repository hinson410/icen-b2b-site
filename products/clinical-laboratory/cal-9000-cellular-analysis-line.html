Product Overview

An all-in-one haematology line, from sample reception to validated report

The CAL 9000 is Mindray’s current generation haematology automation line. The premise is that a large laboratory should not have to move a tube between instruments to get a complete answer: the line delivers CBC, ESR and HbA1c results from a single EDTA tube.

It is assembled from Mindray instruments rather than being a single analyzer. The BC-7600 Series analyzer produces CBC and ESR in about 40 seconds on one instrument; the SC-120 makes and stains slides; the MC-80 performs digital cell morphology with multi-layer fusion imaging; and the H-120 measures HbA1c by HPLC with a stated coefficient of variation at or below 1% at 120 tests per hour.

Around those analyzers sit the logistics: the TM-1000 sample processing system for loading, scanning, registration and archiving, the CR-30[H] concentrated reagent delivery system, and labXpert software. labXpert adds multi-condition re-exam rules, auto-validation of normal samples and multi-station remote validation at no additional licence cost, which is the part that changes how many staff hours the line consumes.

Applications

  • High-volume haematology with CBC, ESR and HbA1c on one tube
  • Automated slide making and digital morphology review
  • Auto-validation of normal samples, with rules-based review of abnormal ones
  • Multi-station reporting across a laboratory network

Who is this product for?

  • Large hospital haematology laboratories
  • Reference and regional laboratories
  • Laboratories processing several thousand CBCs per day
  • Sites consolidating separate CBC, ESR and HbA1c benches

FAQ

Common questions about Mindray CAL 9000 Cellular Analysis Line

What does the CAL 9000 actually consist of?

It is a line, not a single analyzer. The core is the BC-7600 Series for CBC and ESR, supported by the SC-120 slide maker and stainer, the MC-80 digital morphology analyzer, the H-120 HbA1c analyzer, the TM-1000 sample processing system, the CR-30[H] reagent delivery system and labXpert software.

Can CBC, ESR and HbA1c really come from one tube?

Yes. Mindray states that the line delivers CBC, ESR and HbA1c results from a single EDTA tube, which removes the need to draw and route separate tubes for each test.

How fast are CBC and ESR?

The BC-7600 Series produces reliable CBC + ESR results in about 40 seconds within a single instrument, based on a photometric method.

What does labXpert change for staff?

It applies re-exam rules with multiple conditions, auto-validates normal samples and allows multi-station remote validation. In practice that moves staff attention to the abnormal results instead of every result.

Is the line fixed in one layout?

No. Mindray lists outer L, inner L, pillar bypass and turn-module configurations, so the line can be fitted to the room rather than the room being rebuilt for the line.

How is this different from a BC-7600 on its own?

The BC-7600 is the analyzer; the CAL 9000 is the automation line built around it. If you need CBC and ESR only, compare with the BC-7600 Series page first.
