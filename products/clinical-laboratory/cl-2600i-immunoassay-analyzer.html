Typical Applications

  • Clinical laboratories
  • Hormone and tumor marker immunoassay testing
  • STAT and emergency sample testing
  • Laboratories with limited bench space

Key Specifications

  • Type: Immunoassay System
  • Instrument classification: Class II
  • Throughput: 240 tests per hour
  • Footprint: Under 1 m²
  • Sample positions: 70 onboard with 1-minute STAT response
  • Reagent positions: 25, refrigerated at 2-8°C

Common questions about Mindray CL-2600i Immunoassay Analyzer

What is the throughput of the Mindray CL-2600i?

The CL-2600i processes up to 240 tests per hour while occupying a footprint of less than 1 m², with 70 onboard sample positions and a 1-minute response to STAT samples.

The analyzer is designed to be free of daily maintenance, and its long-life modules and low-consumption design help reduce running costs.
