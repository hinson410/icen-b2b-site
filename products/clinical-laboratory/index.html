Clinical Chemistry & Laboratory

ICEN supplies seven Mindray clinical laboratory systems — the BS-240E, BS-360E, BS-380 and BS-480 chemistry analyzers, the C3510 auto coagulation analyzer, the CL-2600i immunoassay analyzer and the CAL 9000 cellular analysis line — for hospital laboratories, reference laboratories and clinical chemistry departments.

Category Overview

Clinical Chemistry & Laboratory supplied by ICEN

The analyzers are specified by throughput, so you can match capacity to test volume. BS-240E runs up to 240 photometric tests per hour (up to 400 T/H with the optional ISE module), BS-380 runs a constant 300 T/H (up to 450 T/H with ISE), BS-360E up to 360 T/H and BS-480 a constant 400 T/H. CL-2600i immunoassay runs up to 240 tests per hour with a 1-minute STAT response and a footprint under 1 m². C3510 coagulation runs 4 independent clotting channels at a maximum of 300 T/H plus 6 optical channels at up to 100 T/H. CAL 9000 is a cellular analysis line pairing the BC-7600, which reports CBC and ESR from one tube in about 40 seconds, with the SC-120 slide maker / stainer and MC-80 digital morphology.
