Product Overview

A spot-check oximeter, not a bedside monitor

The PM-60 is a handheld pulse oximeter. It measures SpO2 and pulse rate, and Mindray files it under vital signs monitoring rather than with the multi-parameter bedside monitors — a distinction worth stating plainly, because the model number invites the opposite assumption.

It runs in two modes. Continuous mode trends a single patient for up to 96 hours; spot-check mode stores up to 4,000 sets of data with automatic patient ID assignment across 99 IDs, which is what makes it usable on a ward round rather than only at one bedside.

The display is a 2.4-inch colour LCD with a pleth waveform, variable brightness and adjustable visual and audible alarms. It runs from either a lithium-ion pack or AA alkaline cells, so a flat battery does not stop a round.

FAQ

Common questions about Mindray PM-60 Handheld Pulse Oximeter

Is this a patient monitor?

No. The PM-60 is a handheld pulse oximeter measuring SpO2 and pulse rate. If you need ECG, NIBP, temperature or capnography, look at the uMEC or BeneVision ranges instead.

How much data does it store?

96 hours of continuous trending for one patient, or 4,000 sets in spot-check mode.

How are patients identified?

Up to 99 patient IDs are supported and can be assigned automatically in spot-check mode.

What batteries does it take?

Either a lithium-ion pack or AA alkaline cells. Mindray does not publish a run time for either option on the product page.

Does it show a waveform?

Yes, a plethysmographic waveform is displayed on the 2.4-inch colour LCD.

What is not published?

SpO2 range and accuracy, pulse rate range, weight, dimensions, battery runtime and ingress protection are not stated on the product page. Ask ICEN for the datasheet, especially if the accuracy specification has to meet a clinical protocol.
