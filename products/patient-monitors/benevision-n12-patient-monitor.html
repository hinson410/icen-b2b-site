Product Overview

A modular bedside monitor with a simplified workflow

The Mindray BeneVision N12 is a bedside patient monitor with a wide-angle HD display showing up to 8 waveforms for a clear view of real-time vitals. The flat multi-touch interface is designed so that about 90% of common operations are reachable in no more than two steps.

iAlarm technology is intended to reduce nuisance alarms, and the system stores trends, alarms and events with up to 48 hours of full disclosure. The monitor accepts the BeneVision N1 monitor/module as a slide-in unit, which supports gap-free bedside-to-transport monitoring continuity - a practical advantage for departments that move patients between the ward, theatre and ICU.

The modular parameter platform includes advanced ECG options such as QT/QTc, atrial fibrillation detection and 12-lead analysis, with BeneVision DMS, eGateway and HL7 connectivity for wider clinical systems. ICEN supplies the N12 to hospitals, monitoring departments and distributors with configuration, documentation and export coordination.

Applications

  • Bedside patient monitoring
  • Continuous vital-sign observation in clinical departments
  • Bedside-to-transport continuity using the BeneVision N1 module
  • Cardiac monitoring with advanced ECG options

FAQ

Common questions about Mindray BeneVision N12 Patient Monitor

What is the key difference between the BeneVision N1 and N12?

The N1 is a palm-size transport-oriented monitor with a 5.5-inch touchscreen that also works as a module inside the N-Series. The N12 is a bedside monitor with a wide-angle HD display showing up to 8 waveforms and a larger modular parameter platform.

Can the N12 use the N1 as a module?

Yes. The N12 accepts the BeneVision N1 monitor/module as a slide-in unit, which supports gap-free monitoring when a patient is moved.

What parameter options are available on the N12?

The modular platform includes advanced ECG options such as QT/QTc, atrial fibrillation detection and 12-lead analysis, with BeneVision DMS, eGateway and HL7 connectivity.
