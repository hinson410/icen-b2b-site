Product Overview

Mobile radiography designed for the whole hospital route

The Mindray MobiEye 700 is a mobile digital radiography system built for bedside imaging outside the radiology department. Its stated battery capability is up to 700 exposures on a single full charge, with motorized drive covering up to 70 km and up to 7 days of operation per charge.

A distinctive feature is the bionic arm, which is designed to offer maximum positioning flexibility in challenging clinical scenarios - useful when imaging patients who cannot be moved or positioned routinely. Imaging uses a wireless flat-panel detector (FPD) specified for high image quality.

The system is described as slim and light with electric-assisted driving covering the entire hospital range, and its battery life is aimed at intensive ICU, CCU and infection-quarantine use where the unit must move frequently between rooms and does not return to a charging point. ICEN supplies the MobiEye 700 to hospitals, diagnostic imaging departments and distributors with configuration, documentation and export coordination.
