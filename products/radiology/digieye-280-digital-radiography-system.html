Product Overview

A floor-mounted DR built around one detector doing two jobs

The DigiEye 280 is a floor-mounted digital radiography system in which a single portable flat-panel detector is shared between the wall stand and the table. Mindray states that positioning follows the same routine as conventional radiography, so staff do not have to learn a new examination sequence.

Because the detector can be lifted out of its dock, patients on a stretcher or in a wheelchair can be examined where they are rather than being transferred. That is the main operational argument for a single-detector room: one panel covers the wall stand, the table and the bedside case.

**Availability note:** Mindray’s own product page for the DigiEye 280 currently states "Sorry, this product is no longer available" and redirects interest to the DigiEye 330. If you are specifying a new room, ask ICEN to quote the DigiEye 330 or 350 instead, or to confirm whether refurbished DigiEye 280 units are available in your market.

Applications

  • General radiography: chest, abdomen, skeletal
  • Examinations of patients who cannot be moved from a stretcher or wheelchair
  • Standing and lying views on one system
  • Replacement of computed radiography in a single X-ray room

Who is this product for?

  • Radiology departments and imaging rooms
  • Community hospitals and primary care institutions
  • Orthopaedic and chest radiography workflows
  • Facilities replacing computed radiography with DR

FAQ

Common questions about Mindray DigiEye 280 Digital Radiography System

Is the DigiEye 280 still available?

Mindray’s own product page states that the product is no longer available and offers the DigiEye 330 as the latest model. Ask ICEN to confirm current availability in your market before specifying it in a tender.

What is the difference between one detector and two?

A single-detector room uses the same panel on the wall stand and the table, and can carry it to a stretcher or wheelchair. It costs less and covers most routine work; two-detector rooms avoid swapping the panel when workloads are heavy.

Can the DigiEye 280 be used at the bedside?

Yes, within the room. The portable detector can be taken to a stretcher bed or wheelchair, which Mindray presents as a way to avoid secondary injury from moving the patient.

What does the console cover?

One-button power on and off, patient information input and retrieval, exposure parameter adjustment, image acquisition with automatic optimisation and post-processing, and DICOM print, transfer and storage.

Do you have the technical datasheet?

Mindray publishes the DigiEye 280 at feature level only. Ask ICEN for the datasheet covering detector size, generator output and kV/mA range for the exact configuration you are ordering.

What should I compare before choosing?

Compare detector size and DQE, generator output, the number of detector positions, table weight limit and whether stitching is needed for full-spine or full-leg studies. The 350 adds bidirectional synchronisation and iStitch; the 680 is a ceiling-mounted system.
