Product Overview

A floor-mounted room where the tube and the detector move together

The DigiEye 350 is Mindray’s floor-mounted DR system for departments that need more than a basic room. Its defining feature is bidirectional synchronisation: the tube and the detector move together, so the panel is already aligned with the beam position instead of being repositioned for each projection.

Movement is controlled from a radio-frequency remote, which means the radiographer can adjust the system from the console room rather than standing beside the table. The flat panel is mobile, so it can be lifted out for projections where the fixed positions do not fit.

Mindray lists iStitch auto stitching as an option, which extends the same room to full-spine and full-leg studies. The operator interface is a 12.3-inch multi-touch screen with an adaptive layout that stays in sync with the workstation, and images can be reviewed and adjusted at the console rather than only after transfer.

Applications

  • Routine and higher-throughput radiography departments
  • Full-spine and full-leg studies with the iStitch option
  • Orthopaedic imaging requiring flexible detector placement
  • Facilities that want tube and detector alignment automated

Who is this product for?

  • Radiology departments and imaging rooms
  • Community hospitals and primary care institutions
  • Orthopaedic and chest radiography workflows
  • Facilities replacing computed radiography with DR

FAQ

Common questions about Mindray DigiEye 350 Digital Radiography System

What does bidirectional synchronisation do?

The tube and detector move together and stay aligned, so the panel does not have to be repositioned for each projection. That shortens positioning time and reduces repeated adjustments during a list.

Do I need the iStitch option?

Only if you take full-spine or full-leg studies. It is listed as optional, so a room without it still covers routine chest, abdomen and skeletal work.

Can the system be operated from the console room?

Yes. Movement is controlled with an RF remote, which Mindray states works even from the console room.

How much weight does the table support?

Mindray states that the floor track bears more than 170 kg. Confirm the patient weight limit for your exact table configuration with ICEN.

How does it compare with the 330 and 680?

The 330 is the entry room; the 350 adds bidirectional synchronisation, a mobile detector and iStitch. The 680 is ceiling-mounted for a wider range of tube travel.

Do you have the technical datasheet?

Mindray publishes feature-level information only. Ask ICEN for the datasheet with detector, generator and exposure parameters for your configuration.
