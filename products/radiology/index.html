Digital Radiography (DR)

ICEN supplies five Mindray digital radiography systems — the DigiEye 280, DigiEye 330, DigiEye 350 and DigiEye 680 fixed DR systems, and the MobiEye 700 mobile radiography system — for radiology departments, ICU and CCU, and mobile imaging services.

Category Overview

Digital Radiography (DR) supplied by ICEN

The range splits into fixed and mobile DR. DigiEye 330 is a fixed wall-stand and table platform aimed at community hospitals and primary institutions. DigiEye 280 is a floor-mounted system where a single portable flat-panel detector serves both wall stand and table, and its low installation footprint suits rooms with limited space and low ceiling clearance. DigiEye 350 is a fully automatic stationary system with synchronised tube and detector movement plus iStitch imaging for full-spine and full-leg studies. DigiEye 680 is a compact ceiling-mounted integrated system with a high-power generator. MobiEye 700 is the mobile option, using a wireless flat-panel detector and a bionic arm, with motorised drive specified for up to 70 km and up to 700 exposures per charge.

ICEN lists 5 models in this category, including Mindray DigiEye 330 Digital Radiography System, Mindray DigiEye 680 Digital Radiography System and Mindray DigiEye 350 Digital Radiography System.
