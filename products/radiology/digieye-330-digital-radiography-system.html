Product Overview

A streamlined DR room aimed at primary and community institutions

The DigiEye 330 is the entry point of Mindray’s current digital radiography range and the model Mindray itself offers when the DigiEye 280 is no longer available. Mindray describes it as a streamlined design for improved efficiency, suitable for diverse clinical settings.

The system pairs a wall stand with a table so that standing and lying examinations are covered in one room, and it is driven from a workstation console with a dedicated diagnostic monitor available as an option. The stated target is a straightforward digital workflow with immediate image preview rather than a long list of advanced imaging features.

Practically, this is the configuration most often specified when a facility is moving from computed radiography or film to digital, or when a secondary site needs a dependable radiographic room without the cost of a ceiling-mounted system.

Applications

  • Routine chest, abdomen and skeletal radiography
  • Standing and lying examinations in one room
  • Outpatient and community imaging departments
  • Migration from computed radiography to direct digital radiography

Who is this product for?

  • Radiology departments and imaging rooms
  • Community hospitals and primary care institutions
  • Orthopaedic and chest radiography workflows
  • Facilities replacing computed radiography with DR

FAQ

Common questions about Mindray DigiEye 330 Digital Radiography System

Where does the DigiEye 330 sit in the range?

It is the entry model of Mindray’s current DR range. The DigiEye 350 is a floor-mounted system with bidirectional synchronisation and iStitch, and the DigiEye 680 is ceiling-mounted with a wider travel range.

Does it cover both standing and lying examinations?

Yes. The room is configured with a wall stand and a table, so standing and lying projections are taken in the same room.

Is a diagnostic monitor included?

Mindray lists the dedicated diagnostic monitor as optional. Confirm whether your configuration includes a reporting-grade monitor or only the acquisition console display.

Can it replace a computed radiography room?

That is the typical use. Moving from CR to DR removes the cassette handling step and shortens the time between exposure and image review.

Do you have the technical datasheet?

Mindray publishes feature-level information only on the product page. Ask ICEN for the datasheet covering detector, generator and exposure parameters for your configuration.

What should I check in a tender?

Detector size and DQE, generator output, table weight limit, whether stitching is required, and whether the room layout fits your existing shielded space.
