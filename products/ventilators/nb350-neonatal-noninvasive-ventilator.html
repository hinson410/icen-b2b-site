Product Overview

Noninvasive support for neonates, synchronised to the infant

The NB350 is a neonatal noninvasive ventilator from Mindray’s NB series. It supports noninvasive modes rather than intubated ventilation, which is the segment where synchrony and interface choice decide whether the support is tolerated at all.

EasySync is Mindray’s synchronisation approach, and Intelligent Oxygen Control (IOC) manages FiO2 delivery. SNIPPV provides nasal intermittent positive pressure ventilation, and the Infant Jet pressure generator is the component that delivers the pressure profile through the nasal interface.

Interface options include a bonnet with headgear and nasal prongs or a nasal mask, and Mindray publishes no numerical ventilation specifications on the product page — modes, pressure ranges, tidal volumes and FiO2 ranges are all absent, so they have to come from the datasheet with the quotation.

Applications

  • Neonatal respiratory support
  • Nasal IPPV in the NICU
  • Noninvasive weaning support
  • Transport within the neonatal unit

Who is this product for?

  • Neonatal intensive care units
  • Delivery rooms and postnatal wards
  • Level II and Level III nurseries
  • Neonatal transport services

FAQ

Common questions about Mindray NB350 Neonatal Noninvasive Ventilator

Is this an invasive ventilator?

No. The NB350 is a neonatal noninvasive ventilator. Mindray does not list intubated modes for it on the product page.

What is IOC?

Intelligent Oxygen Control, Mindray’s closed-loop oxygen delivery function. It adjusts FiO2 toward the target saturation range rather than holding a fixed setting.

What is EasySync?

Mindray’s synchronisation function for following the infant’s spontaneous breaths, which is the main determinant of tolerance in nasal ventilation.

Which interfaces are available?

A bonnet with headgear plus either nasal prongs or a nasal mask.

Can you send the full specification?

Yes. Mindray does not publish modes, tidal volume, pressure range or FiO2 range on the NB350 product page. Ask ICEN and we will send the manufacturer datasheet with the quotation — those numbers should not be taken from a reseller listing.

How does it relate to the SV series?

The SV series covers invasive and noninvasive ventilation across patient types. The NB350 is specific to neonatal noninvasive support, so the two are specified for different beds.
