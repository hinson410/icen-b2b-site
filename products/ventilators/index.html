ICU & Ventilation

ICEN supplies six Mindray ventilator models — the SV70 / SV70S non-invasive ventilator, the SV300, SV600 and SV800 ICU ventilators, and the TV80 and TV50 transport ventilators — for intensive care, emergency and respiratory care departments. SV600 and SV800 both support adult, paediatric and neonatal patients, with a neonatal tidal volume range starting at 2 mL.

Category Overview

ICU & Ventilation supplied by ICEN

The range separates into ICU and transport use. SV600 and SV800 are the trolley-mounted ICU platforms with a backup air-supply blower, on 15.6-inch 1920x1080 and 18.5-inch 1920x1080 touchscreens respectively and supporting adult, paediatric and neonatal patients. SV300 is the lighter ICU unit at approximately 10 kg excluding trolley, covering adults, children and infants from 3 kg. SV70 / SV70S is dedicated to non-invasive ventilation with real-time PEEPi monitoring. TV80 and TV50 are transport ventilators — TV80 rated at 6.5 kg with a hot-swappable battery specified for 11 hours or more, and TV50 at 4.5 kg with a built-in turbine.

ICEN lists 6 models in this category, including Mindray SV70 / SV70S Non-Invasive Ventilator, Mindray SV300 Ventilator and Mindray SV600 Ventilator.
