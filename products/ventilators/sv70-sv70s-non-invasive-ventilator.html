Product Overview

A non-invasive ventilator built around synchrony and measurable patient effort

The Mindray SV70/SV70S is a ventilator dedicated to non-invasive ventilation. Rather than adapting an invasive platform, Mindray builds the device around mask ventilation: synchronisation with the patient’s own effort, leakage from the mask interface, and objective measures of respiratory effort that a clinician can act on.

EasySync analyses pressure, flow and turbine sensing together to align delivered support with the patient’s breathing pattern. On top of that the device offers decision-support tools: real-time PEEPi monitoring without additional sensors or manual manoeuvres, oesophageal pressure monitoring for patient-ventilator synchrony and lung-protective strategy, and HFNC Sight, which integrates the SpO2 module to calculate ROX in real time with a 48-hour trend.

Operationally the SV70/SV70S uses a 15.6-inch capacitive touchscreen, a dedicated mask storage slot, a graphical patient leakage indicator and a tool-free oxygen sensor design. It connects to central monitoring over cable or Wi-Fi, allowing multi-bed ventilator review, and can be integrated with bedside devices for single-bed fusion monitoring.

Applications

  • Non-invasive ventilation in respiratory failure
  • High-flow nasal cannula therapy with ROX trending
  • Weaning and post-extubation respiratory support
  • Ward and high-dependency respiratory monitoring

Who is this product for?

  • Respiratory wards and high-dependency units
  • ICU step-down and weaning areas
  • Emergency departments starting non-invasive support
  • Hospitals centralising ventilator review at the nurses station

FAQ

Common questions about Mindray SV70 / SV70S Non-Invasive Ventilator

Is the SV70 an invasive ventilator?

No. Mindray positions the SV70/SV70S as a ventilator born for professional non-invasive ventilation. For invasive ventilation, compare with the SV300, SV600 or SV800 in the same category.

What does EasySync actually do?

It analyses pressure, flow and turbine sensing signals together to align the delivered breath with the patient’s own effort. Mindray frames this as the basis of comfort non-invasive ventilation rather than a single trigger setting.

What is HFNC Sight?

It is Mindray’s high-flow nasal cannula tool. It integrates the SpO2 module, calculates ROX in real time, and keeps a 48-hour ROX trend so a team can define when high-flow therapy is succeeding or failing.

How is PEEPi measured?

Real-time PEEPi is displayed without additional sensors or manual intervention, so intrinsic PEEP can be followed continuously rather than at isolated moments.

Can ventilator data be reviewed centrally?

Yes. The SV70/SV70S connects to central monitoring by cable or Wi-Fi for multi-bed ventilator review, and integrates with bedside devices for single-bed fusion monitoring.

Where can we get the full ventilation parameters?

Mindray publishes modes, tidal volume and pressure ranges in the SV70 operator’s manual rather than the product page. Ask ICEN for the current manual or specification sheet for the exact configuration you are ordering.
