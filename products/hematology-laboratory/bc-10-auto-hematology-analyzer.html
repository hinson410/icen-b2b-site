Product Overview

A compact 3-part analyzer for small laboratories and clinics

The BC-10 is Mindray’s compact 3-part differential analyzer. It reports a CBC plus a three-part white cell differential in a single run and prints three histograms, which makes it suited to small laboratories, clinics and point-of-care sites that do not need a five-part differential.

Because the official specification sheet is complete for this model, every row in the table below is taken directly from Mindray’s published data — including throughput, sample volume, data storage and the reported parameter list.

    FAQ

    Questions buyers ask about the Mindray BC-10

    What is the difference between the BC-10 and the BC-20?

    Both are 3-part differential analyzers. The official BC-10 and BC-20 pages each publish their own throughput, sample-volume and parameter figures, and this page lists the BC-10 figures exactly as Mindray publishes them.

    How many parameters does the BC-10 report?

    Mindray’s official documentation states 20 reported parameters plus three histograms. Some reseller pages quote a different count, so we publish the official figure.

    Is the BC-10 a 5-part analyzer?

    No. The BC-10 is a 3-part differential analyzer. If you need a five-part differential, look at the BC-5150, BC-5380, BC-5390CRP or BC-760 on this site.

    Can we run the data through your LIS?

    The official specification lists bi-directional LIS connectivity. We confirm the interface configuration against your LIS before shipping.

    Does ICEN supply the official datasheet?

    Yes. Every specification on this page is taken from Mindray’s published documentation, and we send the datasheet with the quotation on request.
