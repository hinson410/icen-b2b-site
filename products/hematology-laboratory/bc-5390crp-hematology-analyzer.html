Product Overview

CBC, 5-part differential and CRP from one tube

The Mindray BC-5390CRP is an automated hematology analyzer with an integrated CRP module. It reports a complete blood count, a 5-part white blood cell differential and FR-CRP from a single blood tube, using about 35 uL of blood. Combining the two measurements removes the need to draw a separate sample and run a standalone CRP test.

CRP is measured by latex immunoturbidimetric technology with two reagents and dual CRP channels, and the analyzer also offers an individual CRP test mode. A built-in thermostat holds the CRP latex reagent at 2-8 C, keeping it in a refrigerated state on board. Results include NRBC# and NRBC% as well as an NLR parameter.

Throughput is up to 60 samples per hour for CBC+DIFF+CRP, which Mindray positions as the fast combined mode. A capillary whole blood mode supports pediatric and geriatric sampling. ICEN supplies the BC-5390CRP to hospital laboratories, diagnostic centres and distributors, and confirms configuration, reagent supply and destination-market conditions before quotation.

FAQ

Common questions about Mindray BC-5390CRP Hematology Analyzer

What does the BC-5390CRP measure from one tube?

It reports CBC, a 5-part WBC differential and FR-CRP from a single blood tube, using about 35 uL of blood.

How is CRP measured?

FR-CRP uses latex immunoturbidimetric technology with two reagents and dual CRP channels.

Is there a CRP-only mode?

Yes. Alongside the combined CBC+DIFF+CRP mode the analyzer offers an individual CRP test mode, which saves hematology reagents.

How is the CRP reagent stored on board?

A built-in thermostat holds the CRP latex reagent at 2-8 C, keeping it in a refrigerated state inside the analyzer.

Is the model called BC-5390CRP or BC-5390 CRP?

Mindray publishes it as BC-5390 CRP, within the BC-5390 CRP / BC-5310 CRP line. It is also commonly written as BC-5390CRP. Both refer to the same analyzer - confirm the exact designation and configuration with ICEN before ordering.
