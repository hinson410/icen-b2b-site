Product Overview

A five-part analyzer where the official specification is still limited

The BC-5120 designation is confirmed in Mindray’s own controls and calibrator documentation, where the instrument is listed as a haematology analyser with a five-part white cell differential.

Mindray does not currently publish a standalone product page or datasheet for the BC-5120, so most of the usual rows — throughput, sample volume, reagents, display, dimensions and weight — cannot be verified against an official source. Rather than copy figures from a related model, we have left them marked as not published. Ask us and we will send the official documentation for your configuration.
