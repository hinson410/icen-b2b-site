Product Overview

A five-part analyzer that reports CBC and ESR from one sample

The BC-760 belongs to Mindray’s BC-700 Series. The family contains two open-vial models (BC-700 and BC-720) and two autoloader models (BC-760 and BC-780), so the BC-760 is the autoloader entry in that range.

Mindray’s official material for the series highlights CBC and ESR results in 90 seconds, optical PLT-H on every CBC and DIFF test, and SF Cube 3D analysis. Where a figure is not published officially we have left it blank rather than quote a reseller number — in particular the reagent, display and certification rows.

    FAQ

    Questions buyers ask about the Mindray BC-760

    Is the BC-760 a single product or part of a series?

    It is part of the official BC-700 Series. Mindray lists the BC-700 and BC-720 as the open-vial models and the BC-760 and BC-780 as the autoloader models.

    Why is this page called BC-760 and not BC-700 Series?

    Because the BC-760 is the exact model designation in the range. The specification table states the official series name as well, so buyers can match either term.

    Does the BC-760 really report ESR?

    Yes. Mindray’s official material for the BC-700 Series states CBC and ESR results from one sample, with the ESR result in about 90 seconds.

    Can I see the manufacturer’s own video?

    Yes — the official Mindray International video for the BC-700 Series is linked on this page.

    Some reseller listings show different specifications. Which should I trust?
