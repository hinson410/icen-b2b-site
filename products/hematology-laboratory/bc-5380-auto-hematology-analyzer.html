Product Overview

A 5-part analyzer for small and medium laboratory workload

The Mindray BC-5380 is an automated hematology analyzer that reports a complete blood count and a 5-part white blood cell differential - 27 parameters in total, including LIC and ALY. A CBC+DIFF report is produced from 20 uL of whole blood in about one minute, and a CBC-only test needs 15 uL, which keeps the analyzer usable for pediatric and difficult-draw patients.

Throughput is specified at up to 60 samples per hour with the autoloader and up to 50 samples per hour in closed-tube mode. The 30-tube autoloader supports random access for continuous loading, and an independent closed-tube and capillary compartment lets STAT and capillary samples be run without interrupting the routine queue.

The differential uses flow cytometry with semi-conductor laser scatter for forward and side scatter, combined with a patented cluster algorithm. Windows-based software covers customizable reference ranges and report styles, automatic cleaning, and day-to-day quality control with 60 QC files and 4 QC programs. HL7 is supported with uni- and bi-directional LIS connection. ICEN supplies the BC-5380 to hospital laboratories, diagnostic centres and distributors, and confirms configuration, reagent and service requirements before quotation.

FAQ

Common questions about Mindray BC-5380 Automated Hematology Analyzer

What does the BC-5380 report?

A complete blood count with a 5-part WBC differential - 27 parameters including LIC and ALY.

How much blood does the BC-5380 need?

20 uL of whole blood for a CBC+DIFF report, or 15 uL for a CBC-only test. 20 uL is also used in prediluted mode.

How fast is the BC-5380?

A CBC+DIFF report takes about one minute. Throughput is up to 60 samples per hour with the autoloader and up to 50 samples per hour in closed-tube mode.

Can STAT and capillary samples be run without stopping the routine queue?

Yes. The 30-tube autoloader supports random access, and there is an independent closed-tube and capillary compartment for STAT samples.

Does the BC-5380 support quality control?

Yes. It stores 60 QC files with 300 data points per file and supports 4 QC programs, and HL7 with uni- and bi-directional LIS is available.
