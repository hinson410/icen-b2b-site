Product Overview

A 5-part hematology analyzer for routine laboratory workload

The Mindray BC-6800 is an automated hematology analyzer that performs CBC with a 5-part differential, with reticulocyte and NRBC options available. It is specified at 125 tests per hour with a 100-tube autoloader, a throughput level intended for routine laboratory workloads rather than low-volume point testing.

Platelet counting uses fluorescence flow cytometry (PLT-O) for accuracy in samples where impedance methods are less reliable. The analyzer also supports body fluid analysis, which extends its use beyond peripheral blood counting.

Result management covers storage of up to 100,000 results with bi-directional LIS connectivity, so the instrument can sit inside an existing laboratory information workflow. ICEN supplies the BC-6800 to hospital laboratories, diagnostic centres and distributors, and confirms configuration, reagent and service requirements, documentation and destination-market conditions before quotation.
