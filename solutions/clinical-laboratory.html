Clinical Laboratory

Clinical Laboratory Equipment Solutions

ICEN supplies clinical laboratory equipment from Mindray for hospital and reference laboratories - hematology analyzers including the BC-20, BC-5000, BC-5150, BC-5380, BC-5390CRP, BC-6800 and the BC-7600 Series), clinical chemistry analyzers including the BS-240E, BS-360E, BS-380 and BS-480), the C3510 automated coagulation analyzer, the CL-2600i immunoassay analyzer and the CAL 9000 cellular analysis line - for laboratories in 150 countries.

Hematology analyzers

The hematology range runs from entry-level CBC screening to high-throughput 5-part differential analysis. The BC-20 covers basic CBC testing; the BC-5000, BC-5150, BC-5380 and BC-5390CRP cover 5-part differential work at clinic and hospital level, with a CRP option on the BC-5390CRP; the BC-6800 and BC-7600 Series sit at the higher-throughput end.

As published, the BC-6800 performs CBC and 5-part differential testing with reticulocyte and NRBC options at 125 tests per hour, uses a 100-tube autoloader, counts platelets by fluorescence flow cytometry (PLT-O), supports body fluid analysis and stores up to 100,000 results with bi-directional LIS. Several models in the range use only 9 to 35 uL of blood per sample, which matters for paediatric and difficult-draw patients.

Chemistry, coagulation and immunoassay

The BS-240E, BS-360E, BS-380 and BS-480 are automated clinical chemistry analyzers for hospital and reference laboratories. The C3510 handles automated coagulation testing workflows, the CL-2600i covers immunoassay testing, and the CAL 9000 is the automatic cellular analysis line.

Laboratory equipment is rarely bought as a single instrument. Chemistry, hematology, coagulation and immunoassay share sample handling, reagents, interfaces and staff, so specifying them as one laboratory plan usually produces a more coherent result than buying instrument by instrument.

How to specify a laboratory analyzer

Throughput and test menu

Match tests per hour and the available test menu to the laboratory's actual workload rather than its peak capacity.

Sample volume and handling

Sample volume per test and autoloader capacity determine how the instrument fits paediatric work and high-volume routine testing.

Reagent system and running cost

Reagent format, open or closed systems and consumable availability affect lifetime cost more than the purchase price.

Data integration

Confirm LIS connectivity - bi-directional where required - before the instrument is installed.

Service and consumables

Response route, spare parts and reagent supply should be part of the same decision.

Documentation and registration

Confirm the documentation set applicable to the destination market before quotation.
